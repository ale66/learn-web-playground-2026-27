Comunicazione pubblica e d'impresa

The master's degree in Public and Corporate Communication at the Università degli Studi di Milano lasts two years and is worth 120 credits. It offers advanced training in the communication of companies, institutions and organisations, combining theory, techniques and the strategic use of information. Classes are held in Milan, admission is limited, and courses are taught in Italian and English.
