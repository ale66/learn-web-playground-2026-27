The Duomo

Milan's Iconic Cathedral

The Duomo di Milano is a magnificent cathedral located in the heart of Milan, Italy. It is one of the largest churches in the world and a stunning example of Gothic architecture. The construction of the cathedral began in 1386 and took nearly six centuries to complete, with its final details finished in 1965. The Duomo is renowned for its intricate façade, adorned with countless statues and spires, as well as its breathtaking interior, which features beautiful stained glass windows and impressive artworks. Visitors can also climb to the rooftop terraces for panoramic views of the city.
